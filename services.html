How It Works

A clear four-step process.

From the first conversation to handover, here's what working together usually looks like.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Requirements

    We discuss your idea, target audience, project goals, required functionality, and any existing designs or references.

  2. 02

    Scope & Planning

    We define the required pages, functionality, technical approach, deliverables, estimated cost, and timeline based on the agreed scope.

  3. 03

    Development & Testing

    I implement the agreed interface and functionality, then test responsiveness, interactions, and essential project flows.

  4. 04

    Review & Handover

    We review the completed work, address agreed revisions, and hand over the project according to the defined scope.
