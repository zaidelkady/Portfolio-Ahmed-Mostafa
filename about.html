My Story

A short look at how I got here.

The path that led to focusing on frontend development and full web products — and where that work is headed.

01

How It Started

My journey into web development started with an interest in how websites are designed, built, and turned into interactive experiences. Over time, I began exploring the tools and technologies that make modern web products work.

02

What I Focus On Today

Today, I focus on business websites and landing pages, interactive web applications, and e-commerce experiences. I work with API integration and data-driven interfaces, and I implement suitable backend functionality for small and medium-sized projects based on the project's requirements.

03

What I'm Working Toward

I'm committed to improving the quality of what I ship — clearer code, better-organized projects, more complete web experiences — and to growing through practical work rather than adding skills to a list.

04

How I Work With Clients

I work directly with clients who need a clear, working web product. That means understanding the requirements first, then building the interface and functionality together, so the result is responsive, coherent, and maintainable.
