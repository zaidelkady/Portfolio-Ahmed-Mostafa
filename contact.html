What Happens Next?

From inquiry to next steps.

Three simple steps — no rigid process, no fixed promises before we've talked.

  1. 01

    You Share Your Idea

    Tell me what you're planning, what you want to achieve, and any requirements you already have. You don't need a complete technical brief to get started.

  2. 02

    We Discuss the Requirements

    We'll discuss the project's scope, required functionality, and any important details to understand whether the project is a suitable fit.

  3. 03

    We Define the Next Steps

    If the project is a good fit, we can discuss the proposed scope, estimated cost, timeline, and the next steps before development begins.
